SPHEREx maps the whole sky every six months in 102 infrared colours.
Pick a target to watch it change over time.

SPHEREx began its survey in May 2025, so its own visits span 2025–2026 (new data arrive weekly). To stretch the time baseline to ~25 years, archival 2MASS (~2000) and WISE (2010) images of the same field are added as extra frames; see also the 1950s–1990s plates in “Sky context & long-term motion” below.

ⓘ

Each SPHEREx visit is compared with the median of the other SPHEREx visits. Archival frames are compared with the SPHEREx median and come from different telescopes.

Detector groups
ⓘ

Choose SPHEREx detector groups by their approximate wavelength ranges. These are not individual selections of all 102 spectral bands.

ⓘ

Selected detector groups are shown as translucent false colours. Colours are illustrative, not natural colours; brightness is scaled for display. Not all groups are available in every visit.

Advanced options
ⓘ

False colour assigns detector groups to display colours, not natural colours. D5+D6 vs D4 may highlight cold sources but does not establish an object's identity. Combine visits uses a median; for a Solar System object, Align & stack aligns frames on its predicted position.

ⓘ

Black and White set the brightness range (percentiles); Asinh compresses bright stars so faint sources stay visible.

ⓘ

2MASS and WISE images come from different telescopes and bands. They are useful for comparing positions over time, but brightness should not be compared directly.

ⓘ

Boxes flag possible motion, variability or artifacts. They are hints, not confirmed discoveries; inspect them carefully.

    Data-quality notes

      How to spot a mover

      • Blink: Look for a point that changes position between visits.
      • Difference: A moving source may appear as a light/dark pair.
      • Boxes: Highlight possible changes, not confirmed discoveries.
      • Check: Artifacts and missing Gaia matches need further investigation.
      • Save: Click a source and choose “Save as candidate” to keep it in this browser.
